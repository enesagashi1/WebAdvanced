Dua Lipa's musical career began in 2015 when she released her debut single "New Love" under Warner Bros. Records, followed by her breakout hit "Be the One" which gained international success across Europe. Early in her journey, she built momentum through consistent live performances, striking visuals, and pop hooks that quickly connected with listeners beyond the UK. Her self-titled debut studio album was released in June 2017, featuring chart-topping singles like "New Rules", which peaked at number one in the UK and made her a global superstar. Alongside "New Rules", tracks like "IDGAF" and "Blow Your Mind (Mwah)" helped cement her place in mainstream pop and earned her major recognition at the BRIT Awards. She later expanded her global reach with Future Nostalgia (2020), earning multiple Grammy Awards, and continued her musical evolution with Radical Optimism (2024). Blending retro disco-pop influences on Future Nostalgia with psychedelic, breezy pop textures on Radical Optimism, she has established herself as one of the defining pop artists and stadium headliners of her generation
