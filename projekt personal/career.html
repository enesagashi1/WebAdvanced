CAREER

Dua Lipa's musical career began in 2015 when she released her debut single "New Love" under Warner Bros. Records, followed by her breakout hit "Be the One" which gained international success across Europe.

Her self-titled debut studio album was released in June 2017, featuring chart-topping singles like "New Rules", which peaked at number one in the UK and made her a global superstar.

2017: Debut Album (Dua Lipa)
2020: Future Nostalgia (Grammy Winner)
