Personal Life of Dua Lipa

Roots, Family & Early Background

Dua Lipa was born on August 22, 1995, in London, England, to Albanian parents from Kosovo, Dukagjin Lipa and Anesa Lipa. Growing up in a household where music and art were part of everyday life shaped her creative drive early on.

During her childhood, she also spent time living in Pristina, Kosovo, before returning to London to pursue her music career. This connection to both London and Kosovo remains an important part of her identity and public voice.

Culture, Advocacy & Highlights

  • Born: August 22, 1995, in London, England
  • Heritage: Albanian roots with strong ties to Pristina, Kosovo
  • Sunny Hill Foundation: Co-founded alongside her father Dukagjin Lipa to support cultural initiatives and charitable causes
  • Interests: Literature, fashion, curating book recommendations (Service95), and cultural storytelling
