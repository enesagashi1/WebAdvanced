Hi, I'm Dua Lipa.

POP SINGER

Dua Lipa is a British singer and songwriter who was born on August 22, 1995, in London, England. Her parents are originally from Kosovo and have Albanian roots. She grew up surrounded by music, and her father, Dukagjin Lipa, also had a musical background. Through her hard work and talent, she became one of the most successful pop singers in the world.
